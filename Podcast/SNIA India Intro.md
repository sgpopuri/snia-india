# SNIA India: Building the Foundations for the Data Decade
### Podcast Discussion Outline — Interview with Co-Founders Rahul Gupta & PK Gupta
*(Target runtime: 30–40 minutes)*

---

## Framing Note

The "Three Storage Decisions India Must Make" segment below uses a **proposed candidate set of three decisions**, mapped to SNIA's own focus areas (Accelerate / Format / Transport / Store / Protect / Optimize Infrastructure). Treat these as a starting hypothesis to pressure-test with Rahul and PK, not a fixed answer — swap freely once you know their point of view.

---

## 1. Cold Open / Intro *(0–3 min)*

- Quick framing: why storage/data infrastructure decisions matter right now (AI buildout, data center boom, sovereignty debates)
- Introduce Rahul Gupta and PK Gupta as SNIA India co-founders

---

## 2. The Founding Story *(3–12 min, ~9 min)*

- What was the state of the storage/data infrastructure conversation in India when SNIA India was founded — was there a gap SNIA globally wasn't filling?
- What was the "why now" moment — a specific problem, conversation, or industry event that triggered starting a chapter?
- Early challenges: getting vendor-neutral collaboration going in a market dominated by a few large vendors; convincing companies to show up
- One story each — a meetup, event, or early win that told you "this is working"
- How has the chapter's membership/character changed from founding to today (e.g., from storage networking crowd to now pulling in AI infra folks)?

---

## 3. Vision, Mission, and Relevance to India *(12–19 min, ~7 min)*

- SNIA global's vision is being the trusted authority on "handling and optimizing data"; mission is vendor-neutral standards + education. Which of SNIA's six focus areas — **Accelerate, Format, Transport, Store, Protect, Optimize Infrastructure** — matter most for India today, and why?
- Is SNIA India's mission identical to the global mission, or does it need an India-specific inflection (e.g., skilling a large developer base, or bridging global standards into a market that's often reseller/integrator-heavy rather than R&D-heavy)?
- How does SNIA India's vendor-neutral positioning play against India's specific mix of MNC capacity centers, homegrown cloud/telco players, and a fast-growing GCC (global capability center) ecosystem?

---

## 4. The Core Segment — Three Storage Decisions India Must Make *(19–34 min, ~15 min, ~5 min each)*

### Decision 1 — Sovereignty vs. Scale
As data localization rules and sovereign-cloud pushes intensify, how should Indian enterprises balance compliance with the efficiency of global hyperscale infrastructure?
> Prompt: *"Is data localization actually a storage architecture problem as much as a policy one?"*

### Decision 2 — Build vs. Borrow
With India investing in domestic compute/AI infrastructure, should storage and data infrastructure follow — building indigenous capability (storage software, DPUs, computational storage) — or is importing best-in-class infrastructure the faster path to AI-readiness?
> Prompt: *"Where does India realistically have a right to build vs. a right to adopt?"*

### Decision 3 — Efficiency vs. Growth
India's data center buildout is accelerating fast for AI workloads. Where does sustainability/power/green storage (SNIA's Emerald work) sit — an afterthought or a hard constraint that will shape decisions?
> Prompt: *"Will power availability, not capital, be the real ceiling on India's data ambitions?"*

*(If Rahul/PK already have their own "three decisions" in mind — e.g., skills/talent, standards adoption vs. proprietary lock-in, or public vs. private data infrastructure — this segment structure holds regardless of which three you land on.)*

---

## 5. Where SNIA India Should Go Next *(34–40 min, ~6 min)*

- Given the shift from "storage networking" to "data" globally, what should SNIA India's next 3–5 years focus on — more standards participation, more education/skilling, more industry-academia bridges?
- Should SNIA India push to get more Indian engineers/companies contributing to global SNIA standards work (versus just consuming/attending)?
- Is there a role for SNIA India in India's AI infrastructure policy conversations — a seat at the table on standards for computational storage, security, or green data centers?
- Closing ask to each: one thing they'd want SNIA India to be known for in 5 years

---

## 6. Close / CTA *(last 1–2 min)*

- How can listeners get involved with SNIA India (events, meetups, membership)
- Thank guests, sign-off

---

## Production Notes

- Since Rahul and PK are both "founders," consider asking the same question to both and letting them riff off each other rather than always addressing questions to both at once — it'll feel more like a conversation than two solo interviews.
- The "Three Decisions" segment is where you'll want the most air time and the least scripting — let disagreement between the two of them (if any) breathe.
