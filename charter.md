# SNIA India 2026-27 Charter

> The vision, the seven program tenets, and the rules of engagement for everything SNIA India ships. This file is the *constitution* — every roadmap entry, ownership decision, KPI, and TWG move traces back to a tenet here.

## Vision statement

> SNIA India is the **vendor-neutral technical voice for storage in India's regulator-driven, India-Stack-scale, AI-native digital economy.**
>
> We earn the right to host **SDC India** by shipping technical artifacts at SNIA-grade quality and contributing to global SNIA TWGs.

## Mission (the 17-month outcome)

By November 2027, SNIA India will have:

1. Shipped **4 podcasts, 4 webinars, 8 blogs, and 2 white papers** (1 SNIA-ratified + 1 vendor-neutral position paper) — all on a published cadence with named owners.
2. Activated **2 SNIA TWG memberships** (Storage.AI, Computational Storage) with at least one India-named global deliverable.
3. Submitted a **charter for an India-led TWG** (working name: *Sovereign Storage Primitives*) to the SNIA Technical Council.
4. Inaugurated **at least 1 university chapter** (target: IIT Bombay CASPER; stretch: IISc).
5. Run **3 quarterly press digests** to Indian technology press.
6. Published the inaugural **State of Indian Storage** annual report.
7. Publicly **announced SDC India** with sponsor commitments on track.

## The seven program tenets

Each tenet has a stable ID. Every artifact in [`roadmap.md`](roadmap.md) traces back to at least one.

### `T-IND-01` Ownership clarity is a precondition

**Statement.** Every artifact (podcast, webinar, blog, white paper, digest, T/E/N session) has **Creator + Reviewer + Approver + Amplifier + Speaker(s)** named *before* work starts. Unowned artifacts are cancelled, not rescheduled.

**Rationale.** The current SNIA India team's stated pain point is unclear ownership of tasks. The plan refuses to perpetuate that pattern. Every row in [`roadmap.md`](roadmap.md) must resolve to five names before a Creator picks up the work.

**Resolution.** A monthly check by Mohan (TC Head) confirms that all artifacts in the next 90 days have all five roles filled. Any artifact still missing a role at T-30 is escalated and either rebooked or cancelled.

### `T-IND-02` India differentiator is the editorial filter

**Statement.** Every artifact must answer the question *"why is this **India's** SNIA story, not a re-run of US content?"* through one or more of:

- **India Stack** (Aadhaar, UPI, ONDC, DigiLocker, Account Aggregator, Bhashini, ABDM, AIKosh)
- **Sovereignty / Regulation** (DPDP 2023 + Rules 2025, RBI, SEBI, IRDAI, CERT-In, Telecom Cybersecurity Rules 2024)
- **Academia / Talent** (IIT / IISc / IIIT / CDAC partnerships, student chapters)
- **IndiaAI Mission** (38,231-GPU sovereign pool, AIRAWAT, Yotta Shakti, Tata Vayu, Jio AI Cloud, etc.)

**Rationale.** A SNIA India program that just relays SNIA US content has no reason to exist. The four anchors above are domains where US-based TWGs structurally cannot originate the technical material — Indian operators have the workloads, the regulatory mandates, and the sovereign hardware footprint.

**Resolution.** The "Why India" line is a mandatory front-matter field on every artifact draft. If it cannot be written in one sentence, the artifact is reframed or replaced.

### `T-IND-03` Standards-track contribution beats commentary

**Statement.** Joining a SNIA TWG and contributing a Public Review comment is a higher-tier outcome than producing a podcast about the same spec. The program is funded against TWG seats first, podcasts second.

**Rationale.** Long-term credibility — the kind that earns SDC India a sponsor — comes from being *named on a global deliverable*, not from producing local content about other people's deliverables.

**Resolution.** Public Review comments filed and India-named global deliverables are primary metrics; podcast downloads are secondary. See [`operations.md`](operations.md).

### `T-IND-04` Diaspora bridge, not dependency

**Statement.** Every quarter features at least one diaspora speaker (Mohit Aron, Anand Babu Periasamy, Jaspreet Singh, etc.), but **India-resident voices headline**. We are building India's bench, not borrowing the US's.

**Rationale.** The cheapest fix for a low-traffic India webinar is to fly in a US Distinguished Engineer. That's also the most damaging fix — it tells Indian operators "the real expertise is over there." Diaspora voices are bridge material; India-resident voices are the long-term franchise.

**Resolution.** ≥70% of headline speaker slots in any rolling 4-quarter window must be India-resident.

### `T-IND-05` Talent pipeline as architecture

**Statement.** University chapters, student SDC scholarships, and an annual *Indian Storage PhD Prize* are not extras — they are the **long-arc strategy** that makes SDC India sustainable beyond 2027.

**Rationale.** SNIA India today has zero university chapters. The Indian academic storage bench (IISc, IIT Bombay CASPER, IIT Delhi, IIT Madras) publishes at FAST/ATC/MICRO/ASPLOS but is structurally disconnected from the SNIA standards community.

**Resolution.** At least 1 university chapter inaugurated by mid-2027; faculty advisor named; one published joint output (faculty + TC member) by SDC India.

### `T-IND-06` Cadence over heroics

**Statement.** A predictable tempo beats an ambitious quarter followed by silence. Four webinars on time matter more than six webinars rushed.

**Rationale.** SNIA India's most consistent failure mode is bursty heroics: launch with a flurry, lose momentum, restart later. The reduced cadence in [`roadmap.md`](roadmap.md) is sized for the smallest realistic active subset (5-6 of 9 TC members) with backups named per artifact.

**Resolution.** ≥85% of artifacts shipped within 7 days of planned date. ≤2 cancellations per quarter. If either threshold is missed for two consecutive quarters, the cadence is formally dialled back at the next TC + Board joint review.

### `T-IND-07` SDC India is the test

**Statement.** Every 2026-27 decision is judged against one question: *does this make SDC India more credible, better-attended, and better-sponsored?*

**Rationale.** Without a marquee event, the cadence becomes background noise. SDC India is the forcing function that turns a program into a movement. The runway is detailed in [`standards-and-sdc.md`](standards-and-sdc.md).

**Resolution.** Sponsor commitment deadlines are hard. If pre-conditions for a physical event are not met, fallback is a virtual edition rather than an under-sponsored physical event.

## Calibration of certainty

Every load-bearing claim in any SNIA India artifact is tagged as one of four:

- `[FACT]` — sourced and current; safe to take to a regulator or customer.
- `[PROJECTION]` — directional, based on roadmaps or trajectory; marked as such.
- `[OPINION]` — a TC member's position; defended but not authoritative.
- `[OPEN]` — unresolved; flagged so the reader knows the field is moving.

This calibration applies to every white paper and every blog. Podcasts may be more conversational but the host (TC member) is responsible for verbal calibration of contested claims.

## Forbidden moves

SNIA India does not:

- Repeat vendor marketing as fact. Vendor claims that are load-bearing get a citation and a hedge.
- Use "revolutionary," "game-changing," or "next-generation" as adjectives without earning them with quantitative evidence in the same paragraph.
- Treat AI workloads as the only workload. India Stack OLTP, regulated workloads, and cost-constrained edge are still ≥80% of the bytes for India-resident operators.
- Allow a single TC member or Khushboo Gupta to become a single point of failure on any artifact.
- Approach Tier 1 speakers without an Approver-signed outreach email.
- Brand the chapter activity around any one vendor. SNIA India is vendor-neutral; T/E/N hosting rotates and white-paper co-authorship is multi-vendor by design.
