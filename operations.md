# Operations — Governance, KPIs, Risks, Meeting Cadence

> How SNIA India runs itself. The clarity of *who decides what, when, and how* protects the program from the ownership-vacuum failure mode the chapter has experienced before.
>
> This file extends [`charter.md`](charter.md) into the operational layer: decision-making, conflict resolution, KPIs, risks, and meeting protocols.

## Governance

### Bodies and authorities

| Body | Composition | Authority | Cadence |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Technical Council (TC)** | 9 members (Mohan Head) | Editorial calendar, themes, speakers, TWG seats, Public Review quotas, white papers | Weekly sync (30 min) + monthly extended (90 min) |
| **Board** | 12 members (Piyush Chair) | Charter, sponsor agreements, SDC India venue + budget, Approver decisions, MOUs | Monthly call (60 min) |
| **TC + Board joint review** | All TC + Board + Khushboo + Ops Buddy | Strategic decisions, KPI review, risk reorder, theme add/drop, charter amendments | Last Friday of each quarter, 1.5 hours |
| **Executive Director (Khushboo)** | 1 person | Operations: amplification, BrightTALK, social, press, sponsor logistics, T/E/N venue | Continuous; reports at all syncs |
| **Operations Buddy** | 1 Board member per quarter | Ops support to Khushboo (3-5 hr/week) | Quarterly rotation per [`roadmap.md`](roadmap.md) |

### Decision modes

- **Consensus** — default for charter, themes, SDC India strategic. All TC + Board agree or stand aside.
- **Lazy consensus** — default for operational decisions. If no objection within 5 working days, decided.
- **Majority vote** — for tied debates. TC Head tie-breaks TC; Chair tie-breaks Board.
- **Approver decision** — per-artifact, within their scope. Can be appealed to TC Head + Chair.
- **Chair decision** — rare; emergency only. Subject to retroactive Board endorsement.

| Decision | Decider | Speed |
|---|---|---|
| Artifact owner assignment | TC Head (Mohan) | 1 week |
| Artifact slip / cancellation | TC Head + Approver | Monthly TC sync |

| White paper outline approval | Approver + SNIA US TC | 2-week window |
| Speaker outreach Tier 1 | Approver (Piyush) | 1 week |

| University chapter MOU | Mohan + Piyush | 6 weeks |
| Charter amendment | TC + Board joint review (consensus) | Quarterly only |
| Crisis / public statement | Piyush + Khushboo | Same day |

### Conflict resolution

Escalation ladder:

1. **Artifact level.** Two TC members disagree → discuss within 1 week → escalate to TC Head if unresolved.
2. **TC Head + Approver.** Joint decision within 1 week, with rationale.
3. **Chair.** Piyush decides within 1 week; communicated to TC + Board.
4. **Quarterly joint review.** Structural matters (theme conflict, charter amendment, SDC direction).
5. **SNIA US escalation.** For brand, IP, or chapter governance issues.

### Conflict-of-interest rules

- TC and Board members declare employer affiliation before decisions affecting that employer.
- A Reviewer cannot review a session from their own employer.
- Creator + Reviewer + Approver cannot all be from the same employer.
- Vendor-neutrality breach is escalated to the quarterly review.

## KPIs

KPI design follows two principles: **standards-track contribution beats commentary** (`T-IND-03`) and **cadence over heroics** (`T-IND-06`).

### Cadence health

| KPI | Target | Threshold |
|---|---|---|
| On-time rate (shipped within 7 days of plan) | ≥85% | <70% for 2 quarters → cadence dial-back |
| Cancelled artifacts / quarter | ≤2 | >3 → root-cause at joint review |
| Backup activation rate | ≤15% | High = primary slate unrealistic |

### Standards-track contribution

| KPI | Target | Threshold |
|---|---|---|
| Active TWG seats | 2 full + ≥3 light-touch | <2 by Q1 2027 → escalate |
| Public Review comments / TWG / quarter | ≥1 | <1 for 2 quarters → seat at risk |
| India-named global deliverables | ≥1 by Q3 2027 | Primary credibility metric |
| India-led TWG charter | Submitted by Jun 2027 | Slip → review scope |

### Reach

| KPI | Target | Threshold |
|---|---|---|
| Webinar registrations / event | 100 baseline → +20% QoQ | <100 → review topic/speaker |
| Webinar attend rate | ≥40% live + ≥60% on-demand in 14d | Below → review timing |
| Podcast downloads / episode (90d) | Match SNIA US median | Set baseline after P-01 |
| T/E/N attendance / meetup | 50-100 | <30 → review host/topic |

### Diversity

| KPI | Target | Threshold |
|---|---|---|
| India-resident headline speakers | ≥70% rolling | <60% → escalate |
| Women headline speakers | ≥30% | Below → recruit |
| Academic speakers | ≥15% | |
| Government/regulator speakers | ≥1/quarter | Long-lead outreach |

### SDC India readiness

| KPI | Target |
|---|---|
| Premier sponsors locked | ≥4 by announcement |
| Technology partners locked | ≥6 by announcement |
| Venue LOI | Before announcement |
| SNIA US Board approval | Before sponsor asks |

### Quarterly review template

Each quarter, the TC + Board joint review works through:

1. **Cadence dashboard** (15 min) — on-time rate, slips, cancellations.
2. **Standards-track dashboard** (15 min) — TWG seats, comments, charter progress.
3. **Reach dashboard** (10 min) — registrations, downloads, attendance.
4. **Diversity dashboard** (5 min) — headline composition.
5. **Risks** (15 min) — walk through active/escalated risks.
6. **SDC India readiness** (15 min) — quarterly checkpoint status.
7. **Open issues + decisions** (10 min).
8. **Operations Buddy handoff** (at quarter-end).

## Top 10 Risks

Each risk has probability (L/M/H), impact (L/M/H), a named owner, and an escalation owner.

### R-01 — Khushboo (ED) operational overload `P:H × I:H = 9`

**Why #1.** Khushboo is the only full-time SNIA India staffer. Without load distribution, everything falls on her.

**Mitigation.** Quarterly rotating Operations Buddy (3-5 hr/week). Explicit "no Creator role" rule for Khushboo. Monthly Khushboo + Mohan sync. **Owner:** Mohan (TC Head). **Escalation:** Piyush.

### R-02 — TC member capacity drop `P:H × I:M = 6`

**Why.** Day jobs at NetApp / IBM / Dell / HPE / Hitachi / Marvell / Mindteck / Nutanix. Indian festival calendar is non-negotiable.

**Mitigation.** Every artifact has a named backup Creator. Monthly TC sync confirms 90-day roles filled. Festival blackouts respected. **Owner:** Mohan (TC Head). **Escalation:** Piyush.

### R-03 — Government speaker cancellation `P:M × I:H = 6`

**Why.** MeitY Secretary, RBI Deputy Governor, CERT-In DG, Bhashini CEO — unpredictable schedules. Last-minute pull-outs are the norm.

**Mitigation.** All government slots double-booked with industry/academic backup. Long lead time (14-20 weeks). Three confirmation touches (T-6, T-4, T-2 weeks). **Owner:** Khushboo + Piyush.

### R-04 — SDC India sponsor commitments slip `P:M × I:H = 6`

**Mitigation.** Hard deadline for sponsor commitments. Virtual edition fallback. Pre-condition gating on announcement. **Owner:** Khushboo + Anil. **Escalation:** Piyush + Mohan.

### R-05 — SNIA US calendar / IP conflict `P:L × I:M = 3`

**Mitigation.** Cross-check with SNIA US calendar quarterly. White paper outlines shared with SNIA US TC early. **Owner:** Khushboo + Mohan.

### R-06 — Indian press uptake slow `P:M × I:M = 4`

**Mitigation.** Outreach to ETCIO, CRN India, Storage Newsletter India. Named PR contact at NASSCOM. Quality-first press digest. **Owner:** Khushboo. **Escalation:** Mohan.

### R-07 — TWG India contribution stalls `P:M × I:H = 6`

**Mitigation.** Monthly TWG sync chaired by Mohan (TC Head). Quarterly comment quotas. In-person attendance budget for key events (Storage.AI Denver, SDC US). **Owner:** Mohan. **Escalation:** Piyush.

### R-08 — University chapter pilot fails `P:M × I:L = 4`

**Mitigation.** Target 1 firm (IIT Bombay CASPER), 1 stretch (IISc). Named faculty advisors pre-vetted. Drop to 1 with post-mortem if needed. **Owner:** Mohan + Sriram. **Escalation:** Piyush.

### R-09 — BrightTALK setup delays `P:L × I:M = 2`

**Mitigation.** Ops sprint with Khushboo + Shiva. Zoom fallback for W-01 if BrightTALK not ready. **Owner:** Khushboo + Shiva.

### R-10 — DPDP Rules 2025 timeline shifts `P:L × I:M = 2`

**Mitigation.** Plan does not over-index on DPDP timing. Sovereignty theme stays relevant either way. DPDP-watch in monthly TC sync. **Owner:** Vinoth + Sunil.

### Risk-watching cadence

- **Monthly TC sync** — Mohan (TC Head) raises newly active risk indicators.
- **Quarterly joint review** — formal 15-min walkthrough; risks scored and reordered.
- **Board emergency convene** — if any High-impact risk activates outside cycle.

### Risk closure

A risk is closed when the lead indicator has been below threshold for two consecutive quarters, the structural cause has been removed, or the risk window has passed. Closed risks are archived (not deleted).

## Charter amendment process

The charter is intentionally hard to change. Amendments require:

1. Written proposal circulated 30 days before quarterly joint review.
2. TC review at the monthly sync preceding the joint review.
3. Board pre-review at the monthly call preceding the joint review.
4. Consensus at the quarterly joint review.
5. Versioning in [`charter.md`](charter.md) with rationale. Tenet IDs are never renumbered.

## Onboarding new members

When a new TC or Board member joins:

1. **Day 0.** Welcome from Piyush + Mohan (TC Head); access to this repo.
2. **Week 1.** 1-on-1 orientation with TC Head (or Chair for Board members).
3. **Week 2.** Read all files; identify themes/artifacts of interest.
4. **Week 4.** Join first weekly TC sync.
5. **Week 8.** Named on at least 1 future artifact as Reviewer or Creator.
