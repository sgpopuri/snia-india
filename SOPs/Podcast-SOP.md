# 🎙️ SNIA Podcast Production Workflow & Abstract Template

This document outlines the end-to-end workflow for identifying speakers, preparing abstracts, managing production schedules, and promoting final episodes for the **SNIA Podcast Series**.

---

## 🧭 Phase 1: Speaker Identification & Coordination
**Owning Group:** Technical Committee (TC)

1. **Selection:** Identify the presentation topic and potential speaker(s).
2. **Outreach Strategy:**
   * **Warm Outreach:** If any **SNIA member** has the speaker in their professional network, that member will reach out directly to secure confirmation.
   * **Cold Outreach:** If no internal network connection exists, **Khushboo Gupta** will manage cold outreach to gauge speaker interest.
3. **Point of Contact Allocation:** Once the topic and speaker are officially finalized, the Technical Committee will assign one **TC Owner** to act as the primary Point of Contact (PoC) for that specific episode.

---

## 📝 Phase 2: Abstract Preparation
**Owning Group:** Speaker & Assigned TC PoC

The TC PoC and the Speaker will collaboratively draft the episode abstract using the mandatory template below.

### 📋 Standard Abstract Template

```markdown
**PROPOSED EPISODE TITLE:** [Enter Title Name]  
**PRESENTER:** [Your Name, Title, and Area of Expertise]  

* **EXECUTIVE SUMMARY:** [1–2 sentence summary of the episode's focus]
* **THE CORE CONCEPT:** [1–2 paragraph detailed abstract breaking down the subject matter]
* **TARGET AUDIENCE:** [Identify the specific professional personas or groups who will benefit from this podcast]
* **LISTENER TAKEAWAYS:** 
  * [Key actionable takeaway #1]
  * [Key actionable takeaway #2]
  * [Key actionable takeaway #3]
```

## 🗓️ Phase 4: Production & Recording Schedule
**Owning Group:** Khushboo Gupta (Overall Production PoC)

Khushboo will coordinate with the TC PoC, Speaker, Diane, Michael, and the podcast host (**Eric Wright**) to schedule and execute the following production milestones:

### 📞 Milestone 1: The Prep Call (15 Minutes)
* **Objective:** Align on logistics, tone, and pacing with host Eric Wright.
* **Deliverable:** Following the call, Eric will send overarching themes and potential interview questions to the participants to help them prepare for the recording session.

### 🎙️ Milestone 2: The Recording Session (1 Hour)
* **Objective:** Record full video and audio components.
* **Target Output:** A polished, edited episode with a final length of **30–45 minutes**.
* **Strict Compliance Rules:**
  * ⚖️ **Vendor Neutrality:** Content must remain entirely objective and vendor-neutral. Brand pitching or commercial indexing is strictly prohibited.
  * 🚫 **No Corporate Branding:** Participants are strictly forbidden from wearing clothing with corporate logos or using company-branded digital backgrounds on video.

---

## 🚀 Phase 5: Publication & Marketing
**Owning Group:** Khushboo Gupta, Marketing Ops, & All SNIA Members

### 🌐 1. Syndication & Distribution
The finalized episode will be published across official SNIA networks:
* **Official Website:** Archived on the [SNIA Podcasts Landing Page](https://www.snia.org/podcasts).
* **Streaming Services:** Syndicated widely on major external distribution networks including Spotify, Amazon Music, and Apple Music.
* **Video Platform:** Uploaded to the [SNIA Video YouTube Playlist](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLH_ag5Km-YUYY7ijH-O8quogfjJ5BSEN5).

### 📣 2. Promotional Strategy
* **Internal Campaign:** Marketing Ops will deploy dedicated promotional strategies to drive traffic to the new asset.
* **Amplification:** **All SNIA members** are required to cross-post and reshare the episode announcements through their personal and corporate LinkedIn professional channels to maximize organic reach.
